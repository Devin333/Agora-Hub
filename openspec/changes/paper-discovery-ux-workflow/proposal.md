## Why

The paper discovery preview has an approved visual direction but search, saved papers, comparison, and reader navigation do not form a complete workflow. Users lose queries when selecting a topic and cannot revisit local selections.

## What Changes

- Combine independent keyword, topic, feature, and publication date filters with removable state and relevance ordering.
- Provide a persistent reading list and a factual comparison view with explicit unavailable states.
- Unify preview entry points and preserve discovery state when entering and leaving the reader.
- Carry home research questions into discovery without claiming unimplemented semantic search.
- Reduce first-viewport overhead, clarify card actions, localize categories, and expose corpus freshness and time-aware recovery.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `paper-discovery-workflow`: Desktop discovery, local paper workspace, and reader continuity over real published paper data.

### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact

Frontend preview, shared paper filtering/API and reader navigation. Legacy paper layout remains unchanged. No backend research runtime, ingestion, or publication changes; no new dependencies.
