## Why

Research currently starts quickly but remains fragmented between questions, groups, paper reading and local report drafts. Users need to resume actual work, reuse material and express common constraints without repeatedly navigating, entering context or organizing records.

## What Changes

- Connect a selected homepage group to new research, with a visible removable destination and a separate explicit choice to use group materials.
- Add mode-specific search constraints that are applied by the real result filters; expose and allow correction of recognized constraints.
- Resume exact paper sections or report drafts from history, preserving original discovery filters and per-account ownership.
- Add explicit all-history/group search scopes, a desktop command palette, expandable favorite shortcuts and reversible archive actions.
- Label restored drafts, support clearing and saved prompt templates, and harmonize desktop text readability while preserving the centered composer and 2x2 module cards.
- Recognize arXiv, DOI and GitHub references and expose real reading, related-search and save-to-group actions. Add PDF upload with actual conversion status, retry and source-faithful reading.
- Turn groups into account-scoped material spaces containing paper/project references, notes and report drafts, with selection-based comparison and report preparation. Preserve explicit guest import and prevent account cross-contamination.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `research-workspace-efficiency`: Continuous research, scoped materials and efficient desktop entry interactions.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This change extends the unarchived homepage research sidebar and reader changes.

## Impact

Next.js homepage/sidebar, history model and synchronization, paper reader progress, paper/project search filters, report preparation and new group materials UI. Account-authorized application services and proxy routes will extend persistence and support safe source ingestion through existing Research parsing/publication paths. No fabricated paper text, provider login, AI report generation or quality-gate bypass is introduced.
